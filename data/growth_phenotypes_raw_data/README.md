# Growth phenotype raw data

Primary measurements behind growth calls in `../known_growth_phenotypes.tsv`.

## Experimental details

From the e-mail thread "Re: Plan for CUE results" (Mary Ann Moran and Christa
Smith, 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-03):

- Medium: MBM with L1 trace metals; one carbon source at 12 mM C-equivalents.
- Biomass: OD600.
- Values: mean of three replicates, minus a blank of the same medium with no
  carbon. Christa offered to send the individual replicates.
- Substrates: acetate, citrate, formate, fumarate, gluconate, glucose, glycerol,
  glycolate, lactate, succinate, xylose.
